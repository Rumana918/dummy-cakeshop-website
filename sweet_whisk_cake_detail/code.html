A Symphony of Nutty & Tart Flavors

The Raspberry Pistachio Dream is our tribute to classic European patisserie craftsmanship with a contemporary artisanal twist. Every morning, our master bakers roast raw Sicilian pistachios to absolute perfection before grinding them into a fine, aromatic flour that forms the soul of our sponge layers.

spa

The Sponge Layers

Ultra-moist, nutty pistachio sponge infused with pure Madagascar vanilla extract and folded with whipped egg whites for an airy, cloud-like texture.

nutrition

The Compote & Buttercream

Hand-simmered whole raspberries provide a delightful tart contrast, balanced expertly against silky, low-sugar white chocolate Swiss meringue buttercream.
